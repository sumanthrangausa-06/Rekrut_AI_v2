## 1. Purpose & users
The AI Observer exists for **anti-cheating / hiring integrity** (Sumanth, 2026-10-13): it protects genuine candidates — a cheater who is hired takes a genuine candidate's job. Candidate-facing framing is therefore **fairness and protection, never suspicion**, and an objection or decline is **never a signal** about the candidate (some genuine candidates are privacy-conscious; deterrence comes from announced presence + integrity events put to human review, never from auto-verdicts). Users: recruiters (enablement + component reports), candidates (notice/consent/objection + one combined feedback report), interviewers (blind human assessment), hiring team (gated audit record).

**One system, three tiers.** The recruiter controls **enablement** (Observer on/off at account/session level, subject to the January cohort allow-list). The candidate controls the **tier**, through their consent state. No tier manufactures a score or an observation it does not have.

## 2. The tiered model (ruled)
- **Tier 3 — Consented AI assessment.** The candidate grants a **separate, specific AI-analysis consent** naming the input class — `transcript_content`, `voice`, or `video_face` (a distinct artefact, working name `observer_analysis`, per input class). Only then may the Observer analyse that class of input and produce its own assessment + individual integrity events. Voice/face inputs additionally require the Gate A capture path (distinct `observer_capture` artefact, re-entry contract fired, cohort, Sumanth's activation approval) and counsel determination **C-05**; the transcript-content variant's classification (incl. the lawful provenance of the transcript) is likewise **C-05**. Integrity observations remain **individual events, listed — never an aggregate integrity score, never an autonomous cheating verdict** (standing 2026-10-10 rule).
- **Tier 1+2 — Presence + combined human feedback.** The candidate has not objected to presence and has not granted analysis consent (declined, or never asked because the session is presence-mode). The Observer attends in a **genuinely no-media mode**: it does not subscribe to, decode, record, transcribe, or measure any media — a provable **no-subscription branch**, distinct from today's post-gate subscription path. Its only output is the attendance record (`analysis_provided: false`). After the human interviewer submits and locks their scorecard, a **composer** turns the HUMAN scorecard materials into **one candidate-safe combined qualitative narrative**. The composer output is never labelled an AI Observer assessment; any combined number in it is a combination of human scores only.
- **Tier 0 — Human-only.** The candidate **objects to presence itself** → no Observer joins, in any mode. Sumanth's standing decline ruling applies: the candidate may take a human interview and the recruiter decides whether to proceed. The objection is recorded as a consent artefact and is **never** surfaced as evaluation data (recruiter surfaces cannot distinguish it — §5).

## 3. Tier-selection state machine (server-authoritative)
Tier is resolved **server-side from artefacts on record**, at session scheduling and re-checked at join (the S-046 gate's checkpoint pattern). No client may assert a tier. **Default-deny survives intact for analysis and capture** — the 2026-10-13 amendment changes only what *presence* requires.

**Inputs:** `E` = recruiter enablement (incl. January cohort allow-list, D-2) · `N` = presence notice delivered in advance, naming the Observer as an AI attendee · `O` = candidate presence objection on record · `A(c)` = analysis consent granted for input class `c` · `G(c)` = Gate A activation state for class `c` (contract fired + C-05 + activation approval; voice/video_face only) · withdrawal events at any time.

| # | State | Tier | Effect |
|---|-------|------|--------|
| 1 | `E` off | — | No Observer, no notice flow, no artefacts beyond the disabled setting |
| 2 | `E` on, `O` recorded | **Tier 0** | Human-only interview; no observer job is created |
| 3 | `E` on, no `O`, `A(c)` granted for ≥1 class, and (`c = transcript_content` with C-05 cleared, or `G(c)` active) | **Tier 3** | Observer analyses only the granted class(es); all other classes remain off |
| 4 | `E` on, no `O`, `A(c)` granted but its class is not yet activatable (C-05 open / contract not fired) | **Tier 1+2** | Consent is recorded, not banked into collection (RL-01); session runs presence-only until its class activates under its own gate |
| 5 | `E` on, no `O`, no `A(c)` (declined / unset / not asked) | **Tier 1+2** | Presence on notice + objection path; composer after lock |
| 6 | `N` not delivered | **Tier 0 behaviour** | No notice, no presence — presence without advance notice is not permitted in any tier |

**Transitions:** tier is fixed at session start from the artefacts then on record. Mid-session changes **only downgrade**: withdrawal of analysis consent → analysis stops, collected analysis artefacts queue for deletion, session continues at Tier 1+2; presence objection mid-session → the Observer leaves immediately, session continues at Tier 0 for its remainder. **No mid-session upgrades** — a new consent is a new artefact taken outside the room, effective for a future session, never under in-room pressure. Withdrawal of recording consent for the interview itself is M10's domain and is unaffected by tier.

## 4. Per-tier data inventory
**Tier 3 — exists:** `observer_analysis` consent artefact(s), per input class (plus `observer_capture` artefact for voice/face); `observer_jobs` row; transcript artefact (content variant — lifecycle per §9, provenance a C-05 item); DEK-encrypted measurements (voice/face, Gate A design, when capture is active); integrity events as individual records; `ai_observer_reports` row (sections per §5). **Never exists:** an aggregate integrity score; a deception label or autonomous verdict; a body-language section (out of the programme until a validation path exists); any measurement for a class not granted.
**Tier 1+2 — exists:** presence notice artefact + acknowledgement; attendance record (join/leave, attended) with `analysis_provided: false` — the January audit spine (S-089); the session's tier record; human scorecards (owned by M10); the combined narrative artefact with **per-statement provenance links to its human sources**. **Never exists:** any media subscription by the Observer (the branch cannot subscribe — §9 evidence); an `ai_observer_reports` row (Observer section state is **derived at read time**, per the S-049a pattern — no row is a normal case); any AI score, AI narrative, or integrity event.
**Tier 0 — exists:** the presence-objection artefact (consent-record class, retained per the M19 consent-receipt schedule; audit surface only). **Never exists:** an observer job, an attendance record (the Observer never joins), a report row, or any tier-derived field on the candidate/application record visible to recruiters.
**All tiers — never exists:** reuse of one tier's consent for another purpose (January/presence consent never satisfies analysis or capture — RL-01); a fallback or heuristic score of any kind (S-082 anti-pattern); tier, objection, or decline rendered as a candidate-quality signal anywhere.

## 5. Per-tier report shapes (S-082 projection contract + S-039 audience split apply throughout)
Renderer rules are unchanged and normative: branch only on per-section `state` resolved at read time via `lib/observerReportStates.js` (no fork, no client derivation); absent metrics are "Insufficient data", **never coerced to 0**; unknown `version` renders as `missing`; events listed individually with **no aggregate computed or rendered**; `transcript_available` false ⇒ no transcript UI.
- **Recruiter surface:** Tier 3 → human assessment + AI sections side by side (S-039 layout) + integrity events listed + **discrepancy surface**: both components visible, disagreement preserved in the record (default resolution rule, §10). Tier 1+2 → human assessment + the Observer region renders the canonical **neutral notice** (no-inference clause carried atomically from `observerReportStates.js`) with attendance facts available via the audit surface. Tier 0 → the **same neutral sentence** as Tier 1+2 — declined, withdrawn, objected, and unknown **collapse to one presentation** on recruiter surfaces, so presence outcomes can never be ranked or inferred from.
- **Candidate surface:** exactly **one combined qualitative report** in every tier (2026-10-10 visibility ruling) — Tier 3 combines human + AI qualitative material; Tier 1+2 combines human material only; Tier 0 is the human feedback alone. **No candidate-visible blended interview score** in any tier; no interview-specific numeric score and no integrity detail reaches the candidate report. Candidate/audit surfaces may name the true consent state (audience split); the underlying **source records are retained separately** — presentation is combined, records are never merged — so audit, DSAR, and correction can always reach the components.
- **Interviewer surface:** **blind** — a scorer cannot retrieve the session's AI section (score, narrative, integrity observations, discrepancy indicators) through UI or API until their own scorecard is submitted and locked (server-enforced, per-person — the S-093 AI-section gate is this rule's implementation home; boxed default, §10).
- **Audit surface (gated):** true tier, true consent states, attendance, `analysis_provided` — named, never collapsed.

## 9. Functional requirements
- FR-M11-1: **Tier resolution is server-authoritative** — resolved from artefacts at scheduling, re-checked at join and at flush (the S-046/S-049a checkpoint pattern); a session's tier and its consent states are recorded, and every downstream surface derives from them (no per-surface re-interpretation).
- FR-M11-2: **Tier 1 no-media branch** — the Observer joins via a branch that *cannot* subscribe: no media subscription, decode, recording, transcription, or measurement code path is reachable in Tier 1 mode. Enablement requires **no-subscription evidence** in the Dormancy-Evidence-Pack style (offered-vs-subscribed counters exactly zero, non-vacuous) — see the gate note for Lead. Attendance + `analysis_provided: false` are its only writes.
- FR-M11-3: **Analysis stays default-deny** — Tier 3 analysis runs only for input classes with a granted `observer_analysis` artefact, and for voice/face only when §7's contract state is active. Unknown/unset/declined ⇒ no analysis job, no capture, no report row (state derived at read).
- FR-M11-4: **Capture contract (Tier 3 voice/face)** — flag-off in every environment until the re-entry contract fires; activation requires the distinct `observer_capture` artefact (RL-01); per-candidate DEK encryption, registry-intent-first, recording-class **30-day** sub-policy (D-1), withdrawal = stop + queue-for-deletion with the ≤2 s kill watchdog on the capture path.
- FR-M11-5: **Measurement honesty** — measurement returns **slice-by-slice, never as a bundle**; voice stress may only ever ship as a meter with uncertainty shown; **deception claims are banned**; body language is out until a validation path exists; **no heuristic fallback scores, ever** (S-082 anti-pattern: legacy length-based fallbacks fabricate scores and must not be inherited).
- FR-M11-6: **Composer (Tier 2)** — runs only after the interviewer's scorecard is submitted and locked; sources are the locked human scorecards (read via the M10/S-093 resolver path, never around it); **every combined statement carries provenance to its human source(s)**; in Tier 1+2 the output is labelled combined human feedback — never an AI Observer assessment — and any combined number is human scores only; in Tier 3 the candidate's combined qualitative report may incorporate AI qualitative material with provenance likewise retained. The combined artefact is a **feedback artefact**: it is not a scoring input and does not feed OmniScore (M12).
- FR-M11-7: **Blindness is server-enforced** — a scorer's access to the session's AI section (any tier artefacts beyond attendance) is denied at the API until that scorer's own scorecard is submitted and locked; the rule attaches to the person-as-scorer, including the recruiter==interviewer case (enforced via scorecard state, not role).
- FR-M11-8: **Discrepancy handling** — where Tier 3 AI assessment and the human assessment disagree, the recruiter surface shows both and the disagreement; resolution is by the accountable recruiter (default, §10) and the disagreement is **preserved in the record — never silently averaged** into a single number.
- FR-M11-9: **Report states** resolve server-side via `lib/observerReportStates.js` only — no forked copy, no client-side derivation (S-039 provenance rule, Quinn-verified); canonical notice/declined copy lives there alone.
- FR-M11-10: **No negative inference** — tier, objection, decline, and withdrawal are consent facts, never candidate signals: they appear on recruiter surfaces only as the collapsed neutral presentation (§5), are excluded from ranking/filtering/matching inputs, and are never shown as an empty box or label whose absence could be read against the candidate.
- FR-M11-11: **Erasure covers every tier's artefacts** — jobs, report rows, capture registry, consent/notice/objection artefacts, attendance records, combined narratives and their provenance links; outcome rows follow Lex's C-L1 resolution path; erasure composes with the migration-252 ledger and the S-077 retention machinery in every tier.

## 10. ▣ Adopted defaults under the 2026-10-13 ruling (Sumanth can flip any by saying so)
- **Blindness lasts until scorecard submission + lock — not permanently.** After lock, peer/AI reveal follows the panel pattern (M10/S-093).

- **Discrepancy resolver:** the **accountable recruiter** resolves human/AI disagreement; the disagreement itself is preserved, never silently averaged (FR-M11-8).
