| **Ghosting** | 42% ghosted 3+ times | Candidates disengage, stop applying |

| **Hidden Scores** | AI scores follow candidates across employers for 330 days | "Algorithmic blackball" effect |
| **No Feedback** | 44% of rejected candidates never hear back | Can't improve, repeat mistakes |
| **Racial Bias** | 26% of Black applicants face adverse AI impact | Systemic discrimination |
| **Scam Prevalence** | Significant increase in fake job postings | Candidates hesitant to engage |

**The Stanford HAI Study (2026):** Analyzed 4 million job applications through one AI vendor. Found:
- 10% of candidates who submit 4+ applications are rejected from ALL positions

- 40,000 more Black and Asian candidates would have advanced if AI didn't discriminate

**The "Credit Score for Jobs" Problem:**

> — Human Offset, Medium (2026)
